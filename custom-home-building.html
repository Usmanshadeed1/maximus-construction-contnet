Custom Home Building, From First Sketch to Move-In Day

Building a custom home is one of the biggest investments you'll ever make, and it should feel exciting, not overwhelming. At Maximus Construction NJ, we guide you through every stage, from the first conversation about your vision to the day we hand you the keys.

Whether you arrive with finished plans or just a few ideas on paper, we work closely with you to create a floor plan that fits your family, your lot and your budget. Open-concept living, a dedicated home office, a chef's kitchen, a spacious primary suite: we design around how you actually want to live.

What's Included

  • Design planning and floor plan development
  • Budget planning and material selection
  • Site preparation and excavation
  • Foundation, framing and roofing
  • Plumbing, electrical and HVAC coordination
  • Kitchens, baths and custom closets in-house
  • Interior and exterior finishes
  • Final walkthrough before move-in

Built Around Your Life

Your home should reflect your lifestyle, not a builder's template. We help you choose layouts, materials and finishes that look beautiful and hold up to everyday life. Because we also design custom closets and supply our own kitchen cabinets, your interiors are planned as carefully as your structure, and you benefit from wholesale pricing.

You're Informed at Every Stage

We believe in transparent communication. You'll receive regular progress updates, and we welcome your input along the way, so the finished home matches what you pictured. From the foundation to the final coat of paint, every step is done with precision and care.

We also build accessory buildings such as detached garages and in-law suites, so your property can grow with your family.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for building a custom home?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the design, permitting and material availability. Generally, it takes between 6 and 12 months from the design phase to completion.

Can I customize the floor plan?

Yes. Every home we build is designed around your needs. We can start from your plans, adjust an existing plan or help you create one from scratch.

Do you handle the interior finishes too?

Yes. Kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, trim, custom closets and cabinetry are all handled by our team, which keeps your project coordinated and on budget.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. We'll meet with you to discuss your vision, budget and timeline, and outline the next steps at no cost.
