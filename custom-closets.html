Custom Closets and Home Storage, Designed Around Your Space

Every home hides more space than it shows. A custom closet or storage system turns cluttered, awkward areas into organized spaces that make everyday life easier, from getting dressed in the morning to finding the right pan in the pantry.

Through Maximus Closets, we design every system around your exact measurements and the way you live. You'll see your closet in a realistic 3D design before anything is built, and our own installers handle the installation. We never use subcontractors.

Premium Materials, Not Particle-Board Kits

Store-bought closet kits sag, chip and wear out. Our systems are built from premium wood laminates with soft-close hardware as standard. Choose wood-look, matte or high-gloss finishes, then add drawers, adjustable shelving, double-hang rods, shoe displays, islands or lighting to match your needs.

  • Workmanship warranty on every installation

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom closet cost?

It depends on the size, materials and accessories you choose. Most custom walk-in closets cost between $3,500 and $12,000 installed. Your 3D design comes with an exact price before any work begins.

How long does installation take?

Most closets are installed in one to two days once your design is finalized. Larger walk-in systems, garages or media walls can take up to three days.

Will I see the design before you build it?

Yes. Every project starts with a realistic 3D design, so you can see your exact closet and make changes before anything is built.

Who installs the closet?

Our own trained installers. We never subcontract installation, so the team that designs your closet is accountable for the result.
