FAQ

Answers to Your Remodeling Questions

What types of projects do you handle?

Kitchen and bathroom remodels, whole-home renovations, home additions, custom homes, outdoor living spaces and custom closets. We also sell kitchen cabinets at wholesale prices. No project is too small or too large.

Do you offer a free design consultation?

Yes. Your design consultation and written estimate are both free, with no obligation. We'll review your goals, measure your space, share layout and material ideas, and give you a clear price before any work begins.

How long does a typical remodel take?

It depends on the size of the job and material lead times. A closet can be installed in a day or two, while a kitchen or addition takes longer. Your estimate includes a realistic timeline.

Can I buy cabinets without installation?

Yes. Contractors, designers and homeowners can buy cabinets directly at wholesale prices, ready-to-assemble or pre-assembled. Installation by our crew is available if you want it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Maximus Construction NJ is fully licensed and insured, so your home and your investment are protected from the first day to the final walkthrough.
