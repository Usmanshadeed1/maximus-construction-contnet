Kitchen Remodeling That Fits the Way You Cook, Gather and Live

Your kitchen works harder than any other room in your home. It's where mornings start, homework gets done and guests always end up. If yours feels cramped, dated or short on storage, a well-planned remodel can change how your whole home feels.

At Maximus Construction NJ, we handle every part of your kitchen remodel under one roof: design, demolition, cabinets, countertops, plumbing and electrical coordination, tile, lighting and the final finish. And because we supply our own cabinets at wholesale prices through Maximus Kitchens, more of your budget goes into the details you'll notice every day.

What's Included in Your Kitchen Remodel

  • Layout planning and a free 3D kitchen design
  • Solid-wood cabinets at wholesale prices
  • Countertop selection and installation
  • Backsplash and tile work
  • Kitchen islands and pantry storage
  • Lighting, fixture and appliance installation
  • Flooring, trim and paint
  • Final cleanup and walkthrough

Better Cabinets, Smarter Budget

Cabinets are often the biggest cost in a kitchen remodel. Since we source them directly, you get solid-wood doors on ½-inch plywood boxes in Shaker, two-tone, traditional and modern styles, without paying retail markup. You can even see sample doors before you decide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the size of the kitchen, layout changes and material lead times. A cosmetic update moves faster than a full remodel. We'll give you a realistic schedule in your written estimate and keep you updated.

Can I see my kitchen design before work starts?

Yes. We create a free 3D layout so you can see your cabinets, island and layout before anything is ordered or built.

Can I buy the cabinets from you and use my own installer?

Yes. Our cabinets are available at wholesale prices with or without installation, ready-to-assemble or pre-assembled.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Your consultation, 3D layout and written estimate are free, with no obligation.
