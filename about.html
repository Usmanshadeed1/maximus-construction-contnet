About Us

About Our Company

Your Trusted Partner in Remodeling and Construction

Maximus Construction NJ is a full-service home remodeling and construction company based in Ewing, New Jersey. For more than 15 years, we've helped homeowners build new homes, add space and transform kitchens, bathrooms and living areas into places they truly love.

Our approach is simple. We listen first, plan carefully, give you a clear written estimate and then do the work the right way. Whether it's a custom-built home, a major renovation or a single closet, every project gets the same attention to detail and the same respect for your home.

Today, Maximus brings three specialties together under one name: construction and remodeling, custom closets and wholesale kitchen cabinets. For you, that means one trusted team, better materials and fewer handoffs.

How It Works

A Simple, Stress-Free Process

Good projects start with a clear plan. Here's how we take you from the first call to a finished space you love.

Free Design Consultation

Tell us what you want to change. We visit your home, take measurements, share design ideas and talk honestly about budget, at no cost.

Design & Estimate

We plan the layout, help you choose materials and finishes, and give you a detailed written estimate, with 3D designs for kitchens and closets.

Build

Our crew gets to work on an agreed schedule. You get regular updates and a direct line to the person running your project.

Final Walkthrough

We walk through the finished space together and fix any detail you're not happy with. The job isn't done until you love it.
