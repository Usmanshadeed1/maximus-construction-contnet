Home Additions That Give Your Family Room to Grow

A growing family, a home office, aging parents moving in or simply wanting more breathing room: there are plenty of reasons to need more space. A home addition gives you that space without leaving the home and neighborhood you love.

Maximus Construction NJ designs and builds additions that look like they were always part of your home. We match rooflines, siding, windows and interior finishes, so the new space blends in seamlessly, inside and out.

Types of Additions We Build

  • Family room and living room additions
  • Bedroom and primary suite additions
  • Kitchen expansions and bump-outs
  • In-law suites
  • Detached garages and accessory buildings
  • Home office additions
  • Covered porches and sunrooms
  • Matching exterior and interior finishes

Seamless Inside and Out

The best additions don't look like additions. We plan the structure, layout, lighting and flow so your new space connects naturally to the rest of your home. Inside, we carry through flooring, trim and finishes for a consistent look from room to room.

More Space, More Value

Adding square footage is one of the most effective ways to improve how your home works for you. A well-built addition makes daily life more comfortable now and can make your home more attractive if you decide to sell later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an addition a better option than moving?

For many families, yes. An addition lets you stay in the neighborhood you love and avoid selling and buying costs, while getting exactly the space you need.

Will the addition match my existing home?

Yes. We match rooflines, siding, windows and interior finishes so the addition looks like it was always part of your home.

How long does a home addition take?

Timing depends on the size and complexity of the addition and the approvals required. We'll give you a realistic timeline in your written estimate.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We'll visit your home, discuss your goals and provide a detailed written estimate at no cost.
