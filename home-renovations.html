Home Renovations That Make Your Home Feel New Again

You love your neighborhood, but your home no longer fits the way you live. Maybe the layout feels closed off, the finishes are tired or there's never enough storage. A thoughtful renovation can fix all of that, without the cost and stress of moving.

Maximus Construction NJ handles single-room updates, multi-room projects and complete whole-home renovations. We plan the work carefully, coordinate every trade and keep you informed, so your home comes back better than you imagined.

What We Renovate

  • Whole-home and multi-room renovations
  • Open-concept layouts and wall removal
  • Kitchen and bathroom updates
  • Flooring, drywall, trim and paint
  • Doors, windows and lighting upgrades
  • Built-ins, closets and storage solutions
  • Outdated or damaged area repairs
  • Interior design and finish selection

Renovate Instead of Relocate

Moving means selling costs, a new mortgage and leaving behind a place you love. Renovating lets you keep everything that's right about your home and fix what isn't. We help you prioritize the changes that make the biggest difference to your daily life and your home's value.

Respect for Your Home While We Work

Most of our clients live at home during their renovation. We plan work in sensible phases, protect your floors and furniture, keep dust contained and leave the work area tidy, so daily life stays as normal as possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I stay in my home during the renovation?

In most cases, yes. We plan the work in phases where possible, protect your living areas and keep the job site clean and safe.

Can you remove a wall to open up my layout?

Often, yes. We first check whether the wall is load-bearing and plan the right support, so you get an open, modern layout that is built safely.

Where should I start if I want to renovate several rooms?

Start with a consultation. We'll walk through your home, discuss your priorities and budget, and recommend a plan that makes sense, whether it's all at once or in stages.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Your consultation and detailed written estimate are free, with no obligation.
