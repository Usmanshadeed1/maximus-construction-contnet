Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between RTA and pre-assembled cabinets?

Both use the same solid-wood doors and plywood boxes. RTA cabinets ship flat-packed for fast, affordable delivery and are assembled on site. Pre-assembled cabinets arrive fully built and ready to install.

How fast can I get a quote?

Send us your cabinet list, floor plan or measurements, and we'll follow up with a detailed proposal and a free 3D layout within 24 hours.

Do you only sell to contractors?

No. Homeowners, contractors, remodelers, builders, house flippers and interior designers all get the same wholesale pricing.

Can you install the cabinets for me?

Yes. Our crew can handle the full installation, or you can use your own installer.
