Bathroom Remodeling That Feels Like a Daily Retreat

A great bathroom makes every morning easier and every evening more relaxing. If yours has dated tile, a cramped shower, poor storage or signs of water damage, a remodel can turn it into a clean, bright space you'll enjoy for years.

From powder rooms and hall baths to full primary bathroom remodels, Maximus Construction NJ designs and builds bathrooms that balance style, comfort and durability. We handle the demolition, layout, plumbing coordination, tile, fixtures and finishing, so you have one team responsible for the whole job.

What's Included

  • Full and primary bathroom remodels
  • Walk-in showers and glass enclosures
  • Tub-to-shower conversions
  • Freestanding and soaking tubs
  • Custom tile floors, walls and showers
  • Vanities, countertops and storage
  • Lighting, mirrors and fixtures
  • Ventilation and waterproofing

Built to Handle Water, Steam and Daily Use

Bathrooms take more abuse than any other room. That's why we focus on what's behind the walls as much as what you see: proper waterproofing, good ventilation and careful tile installation that keep your new bathroom looking great and protect your home from moisture.

Small Bathrooms, Big Improvements

Even a small bathroom can feel open and luxurious with the right layout. Floating vanities, niche shelving, larger tiles and better lighting can make a compact space feel twice the size. We'll show you what's possible for your space and your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

It depends on the size of the bathroom, the scope of work and material selections. We'll give you a realistic timeline in your written estimate and keep you updated throughout.

Can you convert my bathtub into a walk-in shower?

Yes. Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most popular upgrades, creating a safer, more modern and easier-to-clean bathroom.

Do you help me choose tile, vanities and fixtures?

Yes. We'll guide you through your options and help you choose materials that fit your style, your budget and how you use the space.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Your consultation and written estimate are free, with no obligation.
