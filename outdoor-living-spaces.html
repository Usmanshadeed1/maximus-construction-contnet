Outdoor Living Spaces Built for New Jersey Summers

Your backyard can be so much more than grass and a grill. With the right design, it becomes your favorite room: a place for summer dinners, weekend gatherings and quiet evenings by the fire.

Maximus Construction NJ designs and builds custom outdoor living spaces that extend your home and fit the way you like to relax and entertain. From a simple deck to a complete outdoor kitchen with a pergola and fireplace, we handle it all.

What We Build

  • Custom decks
  • Paver and stone patios
  • Pergolas and covered patios
  • Outdoor kitchens and grill stations
  • Fire pits and outdoor fireplaces
  • Outdoor lighting
  • Seating walls and planters
  • Landscaping touches

Designed for How You Entertain

Do you host big summer parties or enjoy quiet nights for two? We plan zones for cooking, dining and lounging, add shade where you need it and position fire features and lighting to keep the space comfortable well into the evening.

Built to Handle the Seasons

New Jersey weather is tough on outdoor spaces. We choose durable materials and build on solid foundations, so your deck, patio or pergola looks great summer after summer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I choose a deck or a patio?

It depends on your yard, your home's layout and how you want to use the space. Decks work well on sloped or raised areas, while patios suit level ground. We'll recommend the best fit during your consultation.

Can you build an outdoor kitchen?

Yes. We design and build outdoor kitchens and grill stations, often combined with a pergola, patio and lighting for a complete entertaining space.

When should I start planning?

Planning over winter or early spring is ideal if you want to enjoy your new space by summer.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Your consultation and written estimate are free, with no obligation.
